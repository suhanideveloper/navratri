Navratri

9 Durgas ki Kahaniyan

1

Maa Shailputri

Day 1 - grey 🤍

Pichhle janm me Sati thi, Daksha ki beti. Daksha ne Shiv ka apmaan kiya to Sati yagya ki aag me bhasm ho gayi. Dusre janm me Himalaya Raj ki beti bani - Shailputri! Tapasya karke fir Shiv ko paya.

🔱 Trishul, Kamal, Nandi par sawar
ॐ देवी शैलपुत्र्यै नमः
2

Maa Brahmacharini

Day 2 - orange 🧡

Shiv ko pati ke roop me pane ke liye 1000 saal tak phool-patte khaye, fir bina khaye ghor tapasya ki. Is tapasya se naam Brahmacharini pada.

📿 Japmala, Kamandal
ॐ देवी ब्रह्मचारिण्यै नमः
3

Maa Chandraghanta

Day 3 - white 🤍

Shadi ke samay Shiv ka bhayanak roop dekh Parvati ne ye roop liya. Maathe par ghante ke aakar ka chand hai, jiski awaaz se rakshas bhaagte hain!

🔔 10 haath, Sher par sawar
ॐ देवी चन्द्रघण्टायै नमः
4

Maa Kushmanda

Day 4 - red ❤️

Jab andhera tha, Maa ne apni muskaan se Brahmand banaya! Ku=chhota, Ushma=garam, Anda=brahmand.

☀️ 8 haath, Suraj ke andar rehti hain
ॐ देवी कूष्माण्डायै नमः
5

Maa Skandamata

Day 5 - blue 💙

Bhagwan Kartikeya (Skanda) ki Maa! Maa-bete ke pyaar ki prateek hain.

👩‍👦 God me Skanda, Kamal par
ॐ देवी स्कन्दमातायै नमः
6

Maa Katyayani

Day 6 - yellow 💛

Rishi Katyayan ki beti bankar Mahishasur ka vadh kiya. Yoddha roop!

⚔️ 4 haath, Talwar, Sher par sawar
ॐ देवी कात्यायन्यै नमः
7

Maa Kalaratri

Day 7 - green 💚

Sabse bhayanak roop! Kaala rang, bikhre baal. Raktabeej ko maarne ke liye ye roop liya. Darne ki baat nahi, ye shubh karne wali hain.

🖤 Gadha par sawar
ॐ देवी कालरात्र्यै नमः
8

Maa Mahagauri

Day 8 - peacock 🦚

Tapasya se rang kaala ho gaya tha. Shiv ji ne Ganga jal se nahalaya to chandni jaisi gori ho gayi! Isliye Mahagauri.

🤍 Bail par sawar, Safed vastra
ॐ देवी महागौर्यै नमः
9

Maa Siddhidatri

Day 9 - pink 💖

Sab siddhiyan dene wali! Shiv ji ne inki puja karke 8 siddhiyan payi aur Ardhanarishwar bane.

🌸 Kamal par, 4 haath
ॐ देवी सिद्धिदात्र्यै नमः
10

Vijayadashami

Day 10 - purple 💜

Jeet ka din! Ram ji ne Ravan ko maara aur Maa ne Mahishasur ko. Is din naya kaam shuru karna shubh hota hai.

🏹 Ravan dahan, Shami patte
