Navratri

9 Durgas ki Kahaniyan

1
Maa Shailputri
Day 1 - orange🧡

Pichhle janm me Sati thi, Daksha ki beti. Daksha ne Shiv ka apmaan kiya to Sati yagya ki aag me bhasm ho gayi. Dusre janm me Himalaya Raj ki beti bani - Shailputri! Tapasya karke fir Shiv ko paya.

🔱 Trishul, Kamal, Nandi par sawar
ॐ देवी शैलपुत्र्यै नमः
2
Maa Brahmacharini
Day 2 - white🤍

Shiv ko pane ke liye 1000 saal phool patte khaye, fir bina khaye tap kiya. Is kathor Brahmacharya se naam pada Brahmacharini.

ॐ देवी ब्रह्मचारिण्यै नमः
3
Maa Chandraghanta
Day 3 -red❤

Shiv baraat me bhoot-pret laye to Parvati ki maa behosh ho gayi. Tab Parvati ne chandraghanta roop liya - maathe par ghante jaisa chand. Ghante ki awaz se asur darte hain.

ॐ देवी चन्द्रघण्टायै नमः
4
Maa Kushmanda
Day 4 - blue💙

Jab kuch nahi tha, andhera tha, tab Maa ne apni muskan se Brahmand banaya. Isliye Kushmanda.

ॐ देवी कूष्माण्डायै नमः
5
Maa Skandamata
Day 5 - yellow💛

Skanda = Kartikeya. Kartikeya ki maa hone se Skandamata. Ye mamta ki devi hain, santan sukh deti hain.

ॐ देवी स्कन्दमातायै नमः
6
Maa Katyayani
Day 6 -green💚

Rishi Katyayan ki beti bani. Devtao ke tej se prakat hui aur Mahishasur ka vadh kiya.

ॐ देवी कात्यायन्यै नमः
7
Maa Kalaratri
Day 7 - black🖤

Shumbh-Nishumbh, Raktabeej ko maarne ke liye sabse bhayanak roop liya. Kaala rang, bikhre baal. Par bhakto ko shubh fal deti hain - Shubhankari.

ॐ देवी कालरात्र्यै नमः
8
Maa Mahagauri
Day 8 -🩶

Shiv ne kaali kaha to gora hone ke liye tap kiya. Ganga jal me snaan karte hi rang bijli jaisa gora ho gaya - Mahagauri.

ॐ देवी महागौर्यै नमः
9
Maa Siddhidatri
Day 9 - pink💖

Sab siddhiyon ko dene wali. Shiv ne bhi siddhiyan inhi se li, tab Ardhanarishwar bane.

ॐ देवी सिद्धिदात्र्यै नमः
